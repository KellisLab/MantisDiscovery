\begin{mdcol}
\mdsec{The question}
A coreset is a small weighted subset of a dataset that should give the same model as the full data. For least
squares, which fits can a fixed subset reach as its weights change? How well conditioned are they, and when
does one subset keep working along the whole ridge path?
\mdgap
\mdsec{How we are approaching it}
\begin{itemize}
\item Describe the reachable fits with a hyperplane arrangement.
\item Set up convex reweighting problems that improve conditioning on a fixed support.
\item Match moments on a Krylov subspace to keep the ridge path.
\end{itemize}
\mdgap
\mdsec{What we have so far}
\begin{progress}
On a fixed support, the reachable weighted least-squares fits and their limits are the bounded faces of a hyperplane arrangement. With $d + 1$ rows they fill the convex hull of the $d$-row interpolants.
\end{progress}
\begin{progress}
A subset keeps the entire ridge path exactly when it matches the response cross moment and the action of the covariance on a Krylov subspace.
\end{progress}
\end{mdcol}
}

\mdsec{Open questions}
\begin{itemize}
