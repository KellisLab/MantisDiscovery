About the lab

Mantis Discovery is a research group within Mantis, at MIT CSAIL. We use AI throughout our research and publish our results as papers on Zenodo.

25+

25+ Researchers in mathematics, machine learning, physics and biology work with us. They include PhD students and olympiad medalists.
